# Episode 001 — "الخريطة المفقودة"

## Character lock
- Name: ليث (Laith)
- Species: lion cub
- Palette: honey-gold fur, cream muzzle, teal scarf with gold paw emblem
- Personality: curious, kind, brave, occasionally impulsive
- Voice: warm, playful Egyptian Arabic, short clear sentences

## First 10-second test scene
Visual: Laith walks through a bright, friendly forest, notices an old map on the ground, pauses and looks at it.
Dialogue (Egyptian Arabic): "يا سلام! يا ترى هنلاقي إيه؟"
Tone: curious, gentle, suitable for preschool children.

## Production workflow
1. Render a silent preview with Blender.
2. Approve character proportions and framing.
3. Record or generate an Arabic voice track with appropriate usage rights.
4. Add subtitles and mix sound using FFmpeg.
5. Expand into the full 3-minute episode only after the test scene is approved.
